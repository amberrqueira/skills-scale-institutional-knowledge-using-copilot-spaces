# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA/Test Engineers ensure that features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria before release. They design test strategies, execute testing, and help identify defects early so product and engineering teams can make informed decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans aligned with acceptance criteria and release goals
- Execute manual and automated tests across functional and regression paths
- Report defects with clear reproduction steps and impact assessment
- Validate fixes and sign off on quality gates before release
- Partner with developers and product leads to improve test coverage and quality processes

### Goals
- Reduce production defects and customer-facing issues
- Provide fast feedback during development and release readiness reviews
- Strengthen confidence in the overall quality of delivery

### Typical Communication
- Test planning meetings with developers, PMs, and product leads
- Daily standups and sprint review checkpoints for risk or defect visibility
- Defect reports, quality summaries, and release readiness updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate fixes and improve test coverage.
- Collaborates with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria are testable and measurable.
- Supports Project Managers by highlighting quality risks, release blockers, and readiness status.

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, mentor developers, and help maintain delivery quality as projects become more complex. They balance short-term delivery needs with long-term technical sustainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture, design standards, and integration patterns
- Review technical designs and implementation approaches for feasibility and risk
- Mentor engineers on best practices, maintainability, and scalability
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Resolve cross-team technical dependencies and escalation points

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Build technical capability across the team
- Reduce technical debt and prevent avoidable rework

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews, mentoring sessions, and project planning conversations
- Risk reviews, dependency mapping, and technical escalation updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to guide implementation decisions and code quality standards.
- Works with Product Managers and Project Managers to assess trade-offs between scope, timeline, and technical feasibility.
- Provides structured guidance that keeps delivery aligned with the project’s business and quality goals.

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical implementation. They document workflows, clarify requirements, and help ensure delivered work aligns with customer and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements and process context
- Translate stakeholder needs into clear user stories and acceptance criteria
- Support backlog refinement and prioritization discussions
- Map business workflows and identify opportunities for improvement
- Validate that implemented solutions meet stated business objectives

### Goals
- Improve alignment between business priorities and delivered outcomes
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and scope discussions
- Increase the likelihood of successful adoption and business value realization

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Backlog refinement and story-writing sessions
- Status updates, decision summaries, and impact reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define value-driven requirements and success metrics.
- Partners with Developers to clarify requirements and expected behavior early in delivery.
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing dependencies, stakeholder needs, and risks connected to scope or adoption.

---

## Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches help teams work effectively, remove blockers, and improve delivery flow. They focus on process health, collaboration, and continuous improvement without replacing product or delivery ownership.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint and planning ceremonies with clear outcomes and healthy team dynamics
- Identify and remove impediments affecting delivery flow or team effectiveness
- Coach the team on Agile practices, communication habits, and continuous improvement
- Help maintain transparency around work, dependencies, and risks
- Support retrospectives and action tracking for process improvement

### Goals
- Improve team productivity, predictability, and collaboration
- Create a healthy working rhythm for iterative delivery
- Support sustainable execution and learning across the team

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremonies such as sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives
- Weekly syncs with PMs and engineering leads on team health and delivery flow
- Follow-ups on blockers, process improvements, and team coaching needs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers by improving delivery cadence and helping resolve process friction.
- Works with Developers and Product Managers to ensure team rituals and priorities remain aligned.
- Ensures stakeholder communication remains clear while protecting team focus and sustainable planning.

---

## Stakeholder Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives provide business context, strategic direction, and approval signals for projects. They ensure that work remains aligned with customer needs, priorities, and organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Share business priorities, constraints, and strategic direction
- Provide feedback on outcomes, trade-offs, and decision points
- Review milestone progress and confirm alignment with organizational goals
- Help resolve cross-functional issues that affect scope, resourcing, or timelines

### Goals
- Ensure projects remain valuable, relevant, and well-supported by key stakeholders
- Improve executive visibility into progress and business impact
- Enable timely decisions and shared accountability across groups

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based status updates
- Approval checkpoints, steering reviews, and risk discussions
- Collaborative planning discussions with PMs and Product Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to shape outcome priorities and success measures.
- Works with Project Managers to confirm expectations, decisions, and delivery timing.
- Provides the business context that Developers and QA teams need to ensure the solution delivers real value.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The roles complement one another: Product and Project Managers align strategy and delivery, Developers and Technical Leads drive execution and technical quality, QA ensures verification, Business Analysts clarify requirements, and Stakeholders and Agile coaches keep the system healthy and aligned.

---

## How these roles interact with one another
- Product Managers define priorities and success criteria; Business Analysts turn those priorities into clear requirements; Developers and Technical Leads convert them into buildable solutions.
- Project Managers coordinate the broader plan and communication, while Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches improve team process flow and execution health.
- QA/Test Engineers validate outcomes against acceptance criteria, and Stakeholder Representatives provide strategic alignment and approval.
- These roles work together to clarify ownership, reduce misalignment, and improve accountability across the project lifecycle.
